NightApp Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 5, 2026 · Last updated: May 5, 2026

NightApp ("the App", "we", "us", "our") values the privacy of our users ("you", "your"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we process it, with whom we share it, and what your rights are when you use the App.

NightApp is designed for users aged 18 and above to discover nightlife venues, match with people at the same venue, and chat with them.

1. Definitions

  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Service: The NightApp mobile application and related services.
  • Data Controller: The NightApp team.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information (during signup)

  • Email address
  • Password (stored hashed, never as plain text)
  • Display name
  • Date of birth (used for age calculation)
  • Gender
  • If you sign in with Google: name, email, and profile photo from your Google account

2.2 Profile Information (optional)

  • Profile photos (up to 3)
  • Bio text
  • Music / drink / dance preferences
  • Your home city and country

2.3 Location Data

  • Foreground location (while the app is open): Used to show nearby venues, verify check-ins, and match you with users at the same venue.
  • Granting location permission is optional; without it, matching features won't work but other features remain available.
  • Location data is processed on your device; only the last known coordinates (latitude/longitude) are stored on our servers.
  • Background location (while app is closed) is NOT collected.

2.4 Usage Data

  • Check-in history (which venue, when)
  • Matches and message history
  • Likes / swipes
  • Followed venues
  • Notification preferences
  • Language preference

2.5 Device Data

  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Operating system (Android / iOS) and version
  • App version
  • Device language

2.6 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Browser cookies (the app is mobile-focused)
  • Third-party advertising trackers
  • Microphone / camera (apart from photo selection)
  • Contacts / address book

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

3.1 To Provide the Service

  • Account creation and login
  • Showing your profile to other users (according to your privacy preferences)
  • Suggesting matches (based on your gender and age preferences)
  • Venue listing and check-in flow
  • Messaging

3.2 Communication and Notifications

  • Push notifications for new matches, new messages, or expiring matches (you can disable these)
  • Account security emails (password reset, email verification)

3.3 Safety

  • Operating the report and block system
  • Detecting fake accounts and fraud
  • Protecting the system from abuse

3.4 Improving the Service

  • Ensuring smooth operation (anonymous error logs)
  • Developing new features

3.5 Legal Obligations

  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Responding to legal processes (court orders, etc.)

4. Sharing Information with Other Users

4.1 Profile Visibility

  • Your profile photos, name, age, city/country, and bio text are visible to users you have matched with or who check in at the same venue.
  • Privacy settings let you control:
    • Hide my age in matches: Your age is hidden from your profile card.
    • Hide my country flag in matches: Your country flag is hidden.
    • Show me in venue list: When disabled, you appear as an "Anonymous" figure in venue lists (still contributing to gender percentages, but profile details hidden).
    • Share my visited places: Whether visitors to your profile can see places you've been to.
  • If you turn off "Open to match", you won't be suggested in venue matchmaking.

4.2 Messages

  • Conversations with matched users are between the two parties only.
  • We do not read your messages, but they are retained in our archive for legal proceedings or report investigations.

4.3 Advertising

  • NightApp shows no ads.
  • We do not share your profile with third-party advertisers.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained as long as your account is open.
  • Matches: Matches stay active for 3 hours for free users and 24 hours for Premium users, then archived. Messages are not deleted in archived matches (your history is preserved).
  • Deleted account: When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days. In case of legal obligations (e.g., fraud investigation), relevant records may be retained.
  • Server backups: Full deletion from backups can take up to 90 days.
  • Report records: Content reported by a user is retained for 1 year after the report is closed.

7. Your Rights (GDPR and Equivalent)

If you are a resident of the EU (GDPR), UK, California (CCPA), or similar jurisdictions, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Find out what data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate data (in-app via Profile > Edit).
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Delete your account (Profile > Delete Account).
  • Right to restrict processing.
  • Right to data portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to object: Object to certain processing (e.g., profile recommendation algorithm).
  • Right to withdraw consent: Disable permissions like location anytime in settings.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us (see Section 12).

8. Account Deletion and Data Erasure

You can delete your account instantly within the app:

  1. Go to the Profile tab
  2. Tap the Delete Account card
  3. Complete the two-step confirmation
  4. Your data starts being deleted

What is deleted:

  • Your profile information (photos, name, bio, tags)
  • Your matches and message history
  • Your check-in history
  • Venues you follow
  • Notification preferences
  • Your auth account

This action is irreversible.

9. Children's Privacy

NightApp is intended for users aged 18 and above. We collect your date of birth at signup and reject registrations from users under 18. If we discover that a user under 18 has created an account, we take immediate action to delete the account.

In the United States, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 in compliance with COPPA.

If you believe data of a child under 18 has been collected, please contact us.

10. Data Security

To protect your data, we take the following measures:

  • Encryption: All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — never stored as plain text.
  • Access control: At the database level, Row Level Security (RLS) ensures each user can access only their own data.
  • Up-to-date infrastructure: Servers receive regular security updates.
  • Breach notification: In case of a data breach, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

No system is 100% secure; we don't claim otherwise but follow best practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. For significant changes, we will:

  • Send an in-app notification
  • Notify you by email
  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page

By continuing to use the app after a policy update, you accept the new terms.

12. Contact

For privacy policy, data deletion requests, or general questions:

  • Email: nightapp.support@gmail.com (domain and email address to be added)
  • GitHub Issues: github.com/ragkara/nightapp/issues (when repo is public)

13. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Under GDPR, we process your data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): Account and profile information for service delivery.
  • Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): Location permission, push notifications.
  • Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): Safety, anti-fraud, service improvement.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): Compliance with legal obligations.
This Privacy Policy is effective as of May 5, 2026.
